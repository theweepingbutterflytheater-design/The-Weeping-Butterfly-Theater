THE PROCESS

We don't believe the artist has to arrive fully formed.

ABOUT THE WBT

Artist over art.
Process over perfection.

The Weeping Butterfly Theater supports artists of all mediums through coaching, creative development, networking, and collaboration with other creative and media companies.

“Theater” is bigger than a stage here. It is the space where an artist learns, experiments, performs, creates, fails, grows, and becomes.

— Miss Hayze
